How it works

Chrome Extension · Your store research engine

Install once, open any Shopify or SFCC store, and inspect the full product catalog in seconds. Anonymous scans power the research heat rankings below.

Install Extension — Free View live rankings ↓
  1. 1

    Install from Chrome Web Store

    Click “Add to Chrome”. Free plan: 3 store scans per device per day. Pro unlocks unlimited scans + Excel export.

  2. 2

    Open a Shopify or SFCC store

    Visit any independent store in Chrome — competitor shops, ad landing pages, or supplier sites you want to research.

  3. 3

    Click the ShopRadar icon

    The side panel opens and auto-detects the platform. Product catalog loads from public store data — no login required on the target site.

  4. 4

    Browse products & pricing

    Scroll the full catalog: titles, prices, compare-at prices, images and variants. No manual scraping or copy-paste.

  5. 5

    Export (Pro) & feed the radar

    Pro members export to Excel. Each scan anonymously contributes research heat to the global rankings on this page.
