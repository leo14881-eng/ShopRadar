# ShopRadar Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-05-19

ShopRadar ("we", "the extension") helps users detect Shopify stores and view product summaries.

## Data we collect

- **Device ID**: A random identifier stored locally in your browser to enforce free daily usage limits and Pro subscription.
- **Store domain**: The hostname of the Shopify site you visit while using the extension.
- **Usage events**: Requests to our API (`https://api.shopradar.uk`) for authentication and quota.

## How we use data

- Provide free tier limits and Pro features
- Process payments via Lemon Squeezy (they receive your device ID in checkout metadata)

## Data storage

- API data is stored on our servers (Vultr, EU/US region as configured).
- Local cache may be stored in Chrome `storage` on your device.

## Third parties

- Lemon Squeezy (payments)
- Cloudflare (DNS/CDN for our API)
